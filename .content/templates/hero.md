# Hero Section Template

Use this template to create hero sections. Copy and modify for your needs.

## Simple Hero (Centered)

```markdown
---
section: hero
variant: simple
theme: light
---

# Your Main Headline Goes Here

A compelling subtitle that explains your value proposition in one clear sentence.

[Primary CTA](#) [Secondary CTA](#)
```

## Centered Hero with Image

```markdown
---
section: hero
variant: centered
theme: gradient
image: /hero-image.jpg
---

# Transform Your Business with AI

Build intelligent applications that scale with our cutting-edge platform.

[Start Free Trial](#) [View Demo](#)
```

## Split Hero (Text + Image)

```markdown
---
section: hero
variant: split
theme: dark
image: /hero-screenshot.png
imageAlt: Product screenshot
imagePosition: right
---

# Build Better Products Faster

Ship features your customers love with our modern development platform. No credit card required.
```
